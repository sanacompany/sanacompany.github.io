Robotics & AI software development

End-to-end robotics and AI software: autonomous navigation, computer vision, teleoperation, fleet management and simulation. Built by SANA.

Autonomous navigation & SLAM

We build robotics navigation stacks on ROS and ROS 2: path planning, behavior trees, sensor fusion (IMU + GPS + LiDAR + cameras) and custom SLAM for environments where off-the-shelf maps don't exist. We've shipped autonomy on terrestrial rovers handling unstructured outdoor terrain.

Computer vision & perception

Production perception pipelines using YOLO (v5–v11), OpenCV, PyTorch and TensorRT. Object detection, segmentation, tracking, depth estimation and OCR. We deploy on edge devices (Coral and embedded GPUs) and in the cloud, with retraining loops that get better as your data grows.

Teleoperation & remote control

Real-time teleoperation interfaces with sub-second latency. Web-based control surfaces, low-latency video streaming, gamepad and Switch-controller input pipelines, and safe handoff between AI autonomy and human operators. Battle-tested on UFB humanoid robots and Earth Rovers.

Fleet management & operations

Manage tens to thousands of robots from a single dashboard. Mission planning, OTA updates, telemetry storage, anomaly detection, role-based access. Built on AWS with the same backend disciplines we apply to SaaS: observability, CI/CD, audit logs and rollback.

AI agents & SDKs for research

We build robotics SDKs for AI research teams: clean APIs over hardware, sim-to-real bridges, training environments and gym-style interfaces. We've also built generative AI agents that drive characters in real-time entertainment products.

Simulation & sim-to-real

Unity, Unreal, Gazebo and Isaac simulation pipelines for synthetic data generation, RL training and pre-deployment validation. We run sim, validate behavior, then bridge into production with confidence.

Tech we use day to day

  • Robotics: ROS, ROS 2, MoveIt, Nav2
  • ML: PyTorch, TensorFlow, ONNX, TensorRT
  • Vision: YOLO (v5–v11), OpenCV, MediaPipe
  • Edge: Coral, Raspberry Pi
  • Sim: Unity, Unreal, Gazebo, Isaac Sim
  • Cloud: AWS (Kinesis Video, IoT Core, ECS, Lambda)
  • Streaming: WebRTC, WebSockets, MQTT
